# Xmoon Comment Assistant - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

## What this app does

This app is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel xmoonbot and its support staff. It reads comments posted on that channel and prepares suggested replies. A staff member reviews each suggestion, and a reply is posted to YouTube only after a person approves it.

## Data we access

With the channel owner's authorization, the app accesses the comments on the xmoonbot channel (comment text, author display name, and date) and posts replies or applies moderation actions as the channel. It does not access any other Google or YouTube data.

## How data is used and stored

Comment text and author display names are stored only to prepare and track replies and moderation actions. They are used for no other purpose. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, except that comment text is sent to an AI service to draft a suggested reply.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the channel. To ask for data to be deleted, contact the address below.

## Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
